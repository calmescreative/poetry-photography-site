# White Lace

*Poetry and photography by Matthew Calmes*

![White Lace photograph](../photos/White%20Lace.jpg)

<div class="poem">

And his mind floated,  
like white lace in evening breezes.

The ornate intricacies,  
intertwined,  
but unfurling,  
opening,  
to witching hour wakes.

For what is left,  
once the petals open  
to speak to the summertime winds?

Silence of course.  
Beneath the cold snow.

</div>
